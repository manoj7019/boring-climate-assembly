About the assembly

What is The Boring Climate Assembly about?

Decisions about climate adaptation are made in closed rooms and students are rarely invited, even though it's your future on the line.

At The Boring Climate Assembly, you get a seat at the table.

Your team steps into the shoes of a State Climate Task Force. You get:

  • ₹10 Crore (simulated budget)
  • 1 Indian State
  • 1 Real-world climate crisis

Four steps

How it works

  1. 1

    Form a team

    3 to 5 students from Grades 8–12.

  2. 2

    Get your state

    Each finalist team is given one of six states by draw.

    See the six challenges
  3. 3

    Build your plan

    Your team prepares three things:

    • Policy brief (1–2 pages)
    • A simple prototype of a digital tool, like a dashboard or alert app (made with Canva, Figma, Google Sites or similar, no coding needed)
    • A budget sheet showing how you'd spend the ₹10 crore

    You'll get two mentorship sessions, one hour each, while you build it.

  4. 4

    Pitch

    On the day, you present for 10–12 minutes, then face a 5-minute jury grill from a panel of climate policy experts, urban planners and civic tech people.

Why register

Why register for The Boring Climate Assembly

  • You get to work on a real problem

  • You learn about how government decisions actually get made, and what they cost

  • You pitch your solutions to people who do this work for a living

  • Links environmental science, civics and design in one project

  • Comes with budget templates and guidelines, so teams know where to start

  • No coding background needed. Judging rewards clear thinking and practical ideas
